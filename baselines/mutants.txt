# Argued surviving mutants.

# A surviving mutant means a test executed the line without constraining it —
# `testing.md` rule 15 calls that the characteristic failure of generated tests,
# and rule 17 says such a mutant is killed or argued.
#
# ⚠️ **Killing is the default.** An entry here is a claim that the mutation
# produces behaviour genuinely indistinguishable from the original, not that
# writing the test was inconvenient.
#
# ⚠️ **This is a list nobody grows quietly.** It is reviewed at every milestone
# boundary, the same discipline `baselines/review.txt` and `baselines/unsafe.txt`
# carry, and a baseline that only grows means the tests are losing.
#
# Format: <file>:<line>:<col>: <mutation>  <reason>
#
# ⚠️ The reason is required. A location on its own suppresses a survivor while
# recording nothing about why.
